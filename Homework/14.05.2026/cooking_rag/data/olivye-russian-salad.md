# Olivye Russian Salad

## Description

A creamy and satisfying Russian potato salad featuring diced vegetables, eggs, and a tangy dressing.

**Tags:** russian, salad, potato salad, cold dish, party food

## Ingredients

- 3-4 medium potatoes, peeled
- 2 medium carrots, peeled
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup canned sweet peas, drained
- 1/2 cup dill pickles or gherkins, finely diced
- 1/2 cup cooked chicken breast or ham, finely diced (optional)
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 lemon, for juice
- Salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Optional: 2 tablespoons fresh dill or parsley, chopped, for garnish

## Steps

1. Place the peeled potatoes and carrots in a large pot. Cover with cold water, add a pinch of salt, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer until tender (15-30 minutes). Drain well and let cool completely. Once cool, dice the potatoes and carrots into small, uniform cubes.
2. Place eggs in a saucepan and cover with cold water. Bring to a rolling boil, then remove from heat, cover, and let stand for 10-12 minutes. Drain hot water, transfer eggs to an ice bath, then peel and dice.
3. Finely dice the cooked chicken or ham (if using) and dill pickles. Drain canned peas thoroughly.
4. In a large mixing bowl, gently combine the diced cooled potatoes, carrots, eggs, pickles, and peas. Add diced chicken or ham if using.
5. Add the mayonnaise to the bowl. Squeeze in the juice from about half a lemon. Season generously with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
6. Gently fold all the ingredients together until well coated with the dressing. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to allow flavors to meld. Garnish with fresh dill or parsley, if desired.

## Notes

Chilling the salad for at least an hour is crucial for the flavors to fully develop. Adjust mayonnaise and lemon juice to your personal taste.
