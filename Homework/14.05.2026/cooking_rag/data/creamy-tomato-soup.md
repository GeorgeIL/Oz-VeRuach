# Creamy Tomato Soup

## Description

A comforting and rich tomato soup made with pantry staples, finished with heavy cream and optional Parmesan.

**Tags:** soup, vegetarian option, easy, comfort food, tomato

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 (14.5 ounce) cans diced tomatoes, undrained
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon paprika
- ¼ teaspoon chili flakes
- ½ cup heavy cream
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Grated Parmesan cheese, for serving (optional)
- Fresh lemon juice, a squeeze (optional)

## Steps

1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened and translucent, about 5-7 minutes. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
2. Stir in the dried oregano, paprika, and chili flakes (if using). Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
3. Pour in the canned tomatoes (with their juices) and chicken broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it gently cook for 15-20 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
4. Carefully use an immersion blender to purée the soup directly in the pot until smooth. (Alternatively, you can transfer the soup in batches to a regular blender, being careful with hot liquids, and then return it to the pot.)
5. Stir in the heavy cream until fully combined. Taste and season generously with salt and freshly ground black pepper. If desired, add a small squeeze of fresh lemon juice to brighten the flavors.
6. Ladle the hot soup into bowls. Garnish with a sprinkle of grated Parmesan cheese, if desired. Serve immediately.

## Notes

This soup can be easily doubled. For a chunkier soup, blend only half of the mixture. Serve with crusty bread or a grilled cheese sandwich.
