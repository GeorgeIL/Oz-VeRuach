# The Very Best Chicken Noodle Soup

## Description

This delicious, hearty chicken noodle soup is perfect for cold season. It's easy to make, filling, good for sore throats, and can easily be doubled to serve a big family.

**Tags:** chicken, soup, noodle, hearty, comfort food

## Ingredients

- 3 large chicken breasts, cut into 2 to 3 inch pieces, seasoned generously with salt and pepper
- 2 tablespoons EVOO
- Standard Mire Poix — chop ½ half medium onion, 2 stalks of celery, and 2 large carrots
- 1 large clove garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 package organic chicken stock
- 1 chicken bullion cube (in 1 cup water)
- 1 ¼ cups of thin egg noodles
- 1 cup frozen sweet peas
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons black pepper

## Steps

1. Cut chicken breasts into large pieces (2 to 3 inch pieces, about same thickness) and season generously with salt and pepper. Braise in olive oil until cooked through. Set aside.
2. In a large pot, heat olive oil on medium heat. Add the diced onion, celery and carrots. Sauté for about two minutes. Add in the garlic, thyme and bay leaves, cook until onions are translucent.
3. Return chicken to the pot. Stir so all the flavors mingle, add salt and pepper.
4. Add chicken stock, bullion cube and water. Bring to a slow boil, then reduce heat to low. Simmer for 30 minutes.
5. Add peas and noodles, cook for ten minutes.
6. Serve with crusty French bread.

## Notes

This recipe can be easily doubled to serve a big family. Serve with crusty French bread.
