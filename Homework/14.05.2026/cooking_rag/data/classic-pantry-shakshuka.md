# Classic Pantry Shakshuka

## Description

A vibrant and flavorful shakshuka made with pantry staples like canned tomatoes, garlic, onion, and spices, topped with perfectly poached eggs.

**Tags:** shakshuka, vegetarian, breakfast, brunch, dinner, easy, pantry

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3-4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (28 ounce) can canned tomatoes (crushed or diced)
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon oregano
- Pinch of chili flakes (or more, to taste)
- Salt to taste
- Black pepper to taste
- 4-6 large eggs
- Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped (optional, for garnish)
- Crumbled Parmesan cheese (optional, for serving)

## Steps

1. Heat the olive oil in a large, deep skillet or cast-iron pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened and translucent, about 5-7 minutes.
2. Stir in the minced garlic, paprika, cumin, oregano, and chili flakes. Cook for another minute until fragrant, being careful not to burn the garlic.
3. Pour in the canned tomatoes. If using whole peeled tomatoes, you can crush them with the back of a spoon. Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer.
4. Season generously with salt and black pepper. Let the sauce simmer gently for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens slightly and the flavors meld. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed.
5. Using the back of a spoon, make 4-6 small indentations or 'wells' in the tomato sauce.
6. Carefully crack an egg into each well.
7. Cover the skillet and let the eggs poach in the simmering sauce for 5-8 minutes, or until the whites are set and the yolks are still runny (or cooked to your preferred doneness).
8. Remove from heat. Garnish with fresh parsley or cilantro if you have it, and a sprinkle of Parmesan cheese if desired. Serve hot, directly from the skillet.

## Notes

If you like a slightly sweeter sauce, you can add a pinch of sugar with the tomatoes. Also, a splash of chicken broth or even water can thin the sauce if it becomes too thick.
