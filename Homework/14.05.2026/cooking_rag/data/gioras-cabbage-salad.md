# Giora's Cabbage Salad

## Description

This is an original cabbage salad in an asian style, sweet salty and refreshing!

**Tags:** asian, quick, easy

## Ingredients

- Half a cabbage
- Red onion 1/2
- Green smith apple
- Black sesame seeds
- Sesame oil
- Olive oil
- Vinaigrette sauce
- Cranberries
- Sweet pecans
- Sugar
- Salt

## Steps

1. Slice the cabbage into thin strips
2. Mush the cabbage for at least 2 minutes until wet
3. Slice the onion into thin cubes and add
4. Slice the apple into half match sticks and add
5. Add black sesame to taste
6. Drizzle a bit of sesame oil
7. Drizzle a bit of olive oil
8. Put a good amount of vinaigrette sauce
9. Add cranberries to taste
10. Put a little bit of sugar
11. Balance with salt to taste
12. Add sweet pecans (not mandatory)

## Notes

The key to the salad is to squeeze the cabbage well.
Can crush the pecans to avoid large chunks in the salad.
