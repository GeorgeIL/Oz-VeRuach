# Lemon Herb Pan-Seared Salmon

## Description

A quick and flavorful pan-seared salmon with a bright lemon-herb sauce, perfect for a weeknight meal.

**Tags:** salmon, seafood, pan-seared, quick, weeknight

## Ingredients

- 2 (6 oz each) salmon fillets
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon dried oregano
- Pinch of chili flakes (optional)
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- Fresh parsley or lemon slices for garnish (optional)

## Steps

1. Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel. In a small bowl, mix together the salt, black pepper, paprika, oregano, and chili flakes (if using). Season both sides of the salmon fillets generously with this spice mixture.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Carefully place the salmon fillets, skin-side down (if applicable), in the hot skillet. Cook for 4-6 minutes, or until the skin is crispy and golden brown.
3. Flip the salmon fillets and reduce the heat to medium. Add the butter and minced garlic to the pan. Cook for another 3-5 minutes, basting the salmon with the melted butter and garlic as it cooks, until the salmon is cooked through to your desired doneness (it should flake easily with a fork).
4. Remove the skillet from the heat. Squeeze the fresh lemon juice over the salmon. Swirl it gently in the pan with the butter and garlic.
5. Transfer the salmon to plates. Spoon any remaining lemon-garlic butter sauce from the pan over the fillets. Garnish with fresh parsley or a lemon slice if you have them, and enjoy immediately!

## Notes

This salmon is wonderful served with a simple side salad, some steamed green beans, or even a quick rice pilaf if you have rice on hand. The lemon-garlic butter sauce is truly delightful!
